# LinkedIn post draft

I recently worked on a customer churn prediction project as part of my learning in Data Science.

What interested me most was the business question behind the analysis: **How can a company identify customers who may leave, and decide where to focus its attention?**

I explored a telecom customer dataset, looked at customer patterns, and built prediction models using Logistic Regression and CatBoost. In my notebook, the models achieved ROC-AUC scores of around 0.842 and 0.846 respectively on a test split.

I also explored ways of grouping customers into risk categories and suggesting possible retention actions. These were learning exercises, not strategies tested with real customers.

This project helped me see how data can support better customer conversations and business decisions. I'm still learning, and I'm especially interested in connecting analytical thinking with real customer needs.
